About SpinClub

A simpler way to do laundry.

We’re building a more convenient laundromat experience with flexible services and monthly membership options.

Our idea

Make laundry easier to plan.

For many students, renters, apartment residents, and busy households, access to a washer and dryer at home isn’t always practical. Traditional pay-per-wash laundry can also make regular costs harder to predict.

SpinClub’s subscription-based laundromat concept is designed to give regular customers a clearer way to plan their laundry spending, with membership options for different routines.

What we’re aiming for

  • Affordable, understandable membership options
  • Convenient access to laundry services
  • Choices for individuals and households
  • Optional services for customers short on time
Who we serve

Built around everyday routines.

SpinClub is intended for people who do laundry regularly and value affordability, flexibility, and convenience.

📚

University students

Flexible laundry options for students balancing classes, shared housing, and a budget.

💼

Working professionals

Monthly laundry services for people with busy schedules and limited free time.

🏠

Renters and families

Convenient laundromat memberships for apartment residents and households with regular loads.

Our approach

More convenience, with clear choices.

SpinClub brings self-service laundry together with optional wash and fold and pickup and delivery services. Membership details, availability, and pricing depend on the location and final business offering.
